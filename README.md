# EmAGI

EmAGI: AI employees that work like real colleagues, not chatbots or workflow tools. One employee takes on any role, many tasks at once: marketing (content, social posts, ad campaigns, client reports), sales and outreach, customer support, operations, admin, research or software development. It works on its own full computer and does the work itself in any software, anywhere a person would, including phone calls, with no integration list to wait for, and works with your team in any messenger or email it already uses.
